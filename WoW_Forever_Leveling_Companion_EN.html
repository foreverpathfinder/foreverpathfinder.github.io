Your characters

Each character keeps its own race, class, build, options, level and checklist: tap to switch between them. Everything is saved on this device.

💾 Save your progress

Your progress is already kept on this device. To avoid losing it when you clear your browser data, browse in incognito or switch phone or PC, download a save file and load it when you need it.

🛡 It is a plain .txt text file of a few KB with your characters, levels and checklists: no programs, no viruses. The content is encoded and only this guide reads it.

📱 Put it on your Home Screen, like an app

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share (the square with the ↑ arrow) → Add to Home Screen. This way Safari no longer deletes your progress after a few days without visits.

Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).

If you open it from the icon and your characters are missing, use 📂 Load save file.

🧩 In-game addon test

The same guide inside WoW: a window with the items for your level, your stage and the next talent to take. It detects your race, class and level by itself; Italian if your game is in Italian, English otherwise.

⬇ Download the addon (.zip)
How to install it

1. Extract the zip: you get the ForeverPathfinder folder.

2. Copy it into Interface\AddOns inside your WoW: Forever folder (create AddOns if it is missing).

3. In game type /fp to open or close the window; drag it wherever you like. Tick the items you have done; the < > arrows change level.

This is a test version, not yet used in game: if the AddOns list shows it as “out of date”, enable “Load out of date AddOns”. If something is wrong, use ⚑ Report an error.
