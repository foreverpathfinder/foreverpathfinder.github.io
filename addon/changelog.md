### 1.10.12

- Professions: every rank row now names the trainer to go to, with the zone, and the map button marks that trainer. For crafting professions it is the nearest trainer who really teaches that rank (Journeyman, Expert or Artisan, from the NPC titles in the game data), not just the nearest one.
- Professions: when you reach a cap (75, 150, 225), that rank row lights up and tells you to go to that trainer to keep leveling; the chat reminder at the cap names the same trainer and zone.
