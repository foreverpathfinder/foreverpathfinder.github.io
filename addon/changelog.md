### 1.10.4

- Route, levels 30–44: every stage now names Forever's real quests, quest givers and hubs, taken from the Questie database (Nov 16) and the Wowhead map.
  - Alliance: Southshore (30–33) or Desolace for Night Elves, then Stranglethorn Vale in two stages (Rebel Camp and Nesingwary, then Booty Bay), then Riverglades from Farholde Keep (38–41) and Sunnyglade, Powderfuse Port and Twilight's Shroud (41–44).
  - Horde: Tarren Mill and Hammerfall (30–33) or Desolace, then Grom'gol in two stages with Report to Rog'mar at 38, then Riverglades from Rog'mar (38–41) and Bolder'ok, Powderfuse Port and Twilight's Shroud (41–44).
  - New checklist steps every level or two, with map pins for the quest givers; their quests tick themselves when turned in.
- Riverglades quests and quest givers are now on the map (they were missing from the databases before launch).
- The zone order from 30 to 44 still comes from untested guides and stays marked 🟡.
