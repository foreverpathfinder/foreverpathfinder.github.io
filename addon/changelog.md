### 1.10.18

- New languages: Spanish and French. The addon starts in your game's language if it is Spanish (esES, esMX) or French (frFR); otherwise English as before. Change it in the Options or with `/fp lang es`, `fr`, `en` or `it`; `/fp lang` alone goes back to the previous language.
- In Spanish and French, quests, NPCs, items, zones and bosses use the names of the game client (from Questie's localization data); spells and talents stay in English.
- Questie links (quests in your log, map buttons, dungeon quest chains, quests under an NPC on the map) also work with the addon in Spanish or French and the game in English: the addon falls back to the English name.
- Tab names with accented letters are now fully uppercase (MÉTIERS).
