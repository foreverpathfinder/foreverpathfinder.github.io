### 1.10.2

- Class tab: new rotations for levels 40 and up, from the most favorited Mobalytics guides (updated Nov 12–14):
  - Warlock: Wrack as filler, Curse of the Elements, Nightfall, Shadowburn at 60.
  - Mage: keep Ice Barrier up (it raises Frost damage), Fingers of Frost with Ice Lance, Missile Barrage.
  - Paladin: Unsealed Wrath with Seal of Command and Seal of Righteousness.
  - Shaman: Maelstrom Weapon, Rage of the Farseer, Water Shield and Call of the Elements.
  - Warrior: Shield Slam priority with Shield Block, Victory Rush and Revenge.
  - Rogue: Slice and Dice Rank 2 at 42 becomes the main finisher.
  - Hunter: Bestial Wrath and two mobs at a time.
  - Druid: full Cat rotation with Shifting Power.
- Dungeons: Scarlet Monastery is now split into its 4 wings (Graveyard, Library, Armory, Cathedral), each with its levels, bosses and quests; the Armory and Cathedral show how to get The Scarlet Key.
- Druid: Ferocious Bite is learned at 24 in Forever (was shown at 32).
- Shaman: Stormstrike has an 8 sec cooldown (was shown as 6).
