### 1.10.13

- Dungeons: clearer names. The button that opens the world map on the entrance is now called "Entrance", and the section with the dungeon layout and its bosses is now called "Map".
- Dungeons: verified entrances. Every waypoint now points to the door you walk in through, checked against several sources (QuestieDB, MapUtils, the meeting stones, the dungeon guides); Krol'dok Stronghold was wrong and now points to the cave at 37.5 37.5 in Riverglades.
- Dungeons with more than one entrance get one button each under "How to get there": Uldaman (Dustwind Gulch back door), Blackrock Mountain (Burning Steppes and Searing Gorge), Dire Maul (North, West, East) and Stratholme (Main Gate and Service Gate).
