# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The website is a static page for phones. The addon is a World of Warcraft: Forever addon written in Lua 5.1; it has its own surface brief constraints (see Capabilities and Constraints).

## Users

Players leveling a character from 1 to 60 in World of Warcraft: Forever, mostly Italian speakers (the site and the addon are also in English). They are in the middle of playing: one hand on the keyboard, attention on the game world. They open the addon window when they need an answer (what do I do now, which talent, which quests before a dungeon), read it, and close it again to keep playing. The owner plays with the addon himself and judges every interface by using it, not by looking at a presentation.

## Product Purpose

Forever Pathfinder is a 1–60 leveling guide for every race and class: a website to keep next to the PC and an in-game addon that brings the same guide into the game. Success is a player who always knows the next thing to do without reading a wall of text.

## Positioning

The guide is built from sources ranked by player votes (Mobalytics favorites) and from live game data (Wowhead, Questie database), with every piece marked as confirmed by several sources, single-source, or new in Forever. The addon knows the character (race, class, level, quest log, talents, professions) and shows only what applies to it.

## Operating Context

- In the game: a window opened from a minimap icon or `/fp`, on top of the game world, then closed. World map, quest log, talent window and trainers are linked to it (waypoints, quests ticking themselves, talent highlight).
- Works with Questie (required), OneForAll and MapUtils (optional).
- Tabs today: Percorso (route), Talent, Classe, Profession, Dungeon, Alt, Altro, Info, Opzioni.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The addon window is drawn by code in `addon/ForeverPathfinder/Core.lua`: flat color textures, 1px lines, font strings, game icons by name. No HTML or CSS. Rounded corners, gradients and patterns need texture files generated by the project; game art can be referenced by path but Blizzard files are never copied into the zip.
- Bundled fonts today: Cinzel (titles) and Inter (text), both OFL. Other OFL fonts can be bundled.
- Lua allows 200 local variables per file: UI state lives in tables and `do … end` blocks.
- Every text exists in Italian and English; game terms stay in English.
- Known problems reported by the owner (Nov 14, 2026): the next thing to do does not stand out; the text is dense and everything looks the same weight.
- The redesign of Nov 2026 covers the addon only. The website keeps its current look for now.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Forever Pathfinder. Icon: the addon's own icon (`icon.tga`).
- The owner asked that the new addon interface must not look like a typical AI-made project, must use different colors from the current navy, gold and ice blue, and must feel more harmonious and user friendly.
- Reliability marks stay: ✔ several sources, 🟡 one source or pre-launch, ★ new in Forever.
